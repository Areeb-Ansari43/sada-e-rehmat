Hajj, day by day.

Most pilgrims from the UK and Pakistan perform Hajj al-Tamattu': an Umrah first, then the rites of Hajj from the 8th to the 12th or 13th of Dhul Hijjah. Projected dates for 1448 AH are below. They can move by a day once the moon is sighted.

In short

  • Projected Hajj 2027: 8 Dhul Hijjah = Friday 14 May, Arafat = Saturday 15 May, Eid al-Adha = Sunday 16 May.
  • The pillars of Hajj are Ihram, standing at Arafat, Tawaf al-Ifadah and Sa'i. Missing Arafat means missing Hajj.
  • Pilgrims on Tamattu' must offer a sacrifice (hady). Qurbani is not included in most package prices, including ours.
  • Expect 15–20 km of walking across the five days, in heat of around 40°C in May.

Step by step

حَجٌّ مَبْرُورٌḤajj mabrūr · an accepted Hajj
01/07
Arrival

Perform your Umrah

On Tamattu', you arrive in Makkah in Ihram, perform Umrah, then come out of Ihram and wait for the days of Hajj. If you travel to Madinah first, you put on Ihram before leaving Madinah for Makkah.

Umrah firstExit Ihram until the 8th
Day 1

Tarwiyah: Ihram again, then Mina

Bathe, put on Ihram from your hotel or Aziziya and make the intention for Hajj: Labbayk Allāhumma ḥajjan. Travel to Mina and pray Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha and the next Fajr there, shortening the four-unit prayers without combining them.

Ihram for HajjFive prayers in MinaTalbiyah throughout
Day 2

Arafat: the heart of Hajj

After sunrise, move to Arafat. Pray Dhuhr and Asr shortened and combined at the time of Dhuhr, then spend the afternoon in duʿā', facing the Qibla. Make sure you are within the boundaries of Arafat. Stay until sunset; this day is Hajj.

Dhuhr + Asr combinedStay until sunsetMake duʿā'
Night

Muzdalifah under the open sky

After sunset, travel to Muzdalifah. Pray Maghrib and Isha combined on arrival, rest, and collect pebbles (49 or 70, the size of a chickpea). Pray Fajr there and make duʿā' until it is light. The elderly, women and those caring for them may leave after midnight.

Maghrib + Isha combinedCollect pebblesFajr in Muzdalifah
Day 3

The busiest day of Hajj

Stone Jamarat al-Aqabah with seven pebbles, saying Allāhu akbar with each, and stop the Talbiyah. Then the sacrifice, shaving or trimming, and Tawaf al-Ifadah with Sa'i. After shaving you may leave Ihram clothing. The order can vary if needed.

7 pebbles at AqabahSacrificeShave or trimTawaf al-Ifadah + Sa'i
Days 4–5

The days of Tashreeq

Spend the nights in Mina. Each day after midday, stone all three Jamarat in order, small, middle and large, with seven pebbles each. If you leave on the 12th, you must leave Mina before sunset; otherwise stay and stone again on the 13th.

21 pebbles a dayAfter middayLeave Mina before sunset on the 12th
Farewell

Tawaf al-Wada'

The last thing you do in Makkah is the farewell Tawaf: seven circuits, no Sa'i. Women who are menstruating are excused from it. Then your Hajj is complete.

Last act in MakkahNo Sa'i

Pillars, obligations and sunnahs

It helps to know what is essential and what is recommended, because on a crowded day you may need to prioritise.

Pillars (arkān)

  • Ihram with intention
  • Standing at Arafat
  • Tawaf al-Ifadah
  • Sa'i

Missing a pillar invalidates Hajj or must be made up.

Obligations (wājibāt)

  • Ihram from the Miqat
  • Staying at Arafat until sunset
  • The night in Muzdalifah
  • Nights in Mina on the 11th and 12th
  • Stoning the Jamarat
  • Shaving or trimming
  • Farewell Tawaf
Different schools, different lists

The four schools of Islamic law classify a few acts differently, and the penalty for missing an obligation is usually a sacrifice. Ask your group's scholar before Hajj so you know what applies to you.

Making the most of Arafat

The afternoon of Arafat is the most important time of your Hajj. Rest in the morning so you have energy for the hours after Dhuhr. Write a list of the people and the things you want to pray for before you leave home.

The best duʿā' · the Day of Arafah
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamd, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr.
There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things.
Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī: “The best duʿā' is the duʿā' of the Day of Arafah.”
  • Check you are inside the Arafat boundary signs
  • Keep a small umbrella and a water bottle with you
  • Stay in your tent or shade at the hottest time
  • Avoid climbing Jabal al-Rahmah in the heat; it is not required
  • Do not leave before sunset

Stoning the Jamarat safely

The Jamarat Bridge has several levels and one-way flows, which has made stoning far safer than it used to be. Your Maktab will usually be given set times; follow them. Pebbles should hit the basin around the pillar, not necessarily the pillar itself.

  • Stone after midday on the 11th, 12th and 13th, in the order: small, middle, large.
  • After the small and middle Jamarat, step aside, face the Qibla and make a long duʿā'.
  • The elderly or unwell may appoint someone to stone on their behalf, after that person has stoned for themselves.

Staying well during Hajj

May temperatures in Makkah regularly reach 40–45°C. Heat illness is the biggest risk to pilgrims. Drink before you feel thirsty, use oral rehydration salts, walk at night where you can, and rest whenever you are told to. Break in your sandals well before the trip. See our health guide.

Questions

When is Hajj 2027?

Hajj 1448 AH is projected for 14–19 May 2027, with the Day of Arafah on Saturday 15 May and Eid al-Adha on Sunday 16 May. Dates are confirmed by moon sighting and can move by one day.

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah?

Hajj is obligatory once in a lifetime for those able, happens only on 8–13 Dhul Hijjah and includes Arafat, Muzdalifah, Mina and the Jamarat. Umrah is shorter, can be performed almost any time of year and consists of Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i and cutting the hair.

How many pebbles do I need?

49 if you leave Mina on the 12th (7 on the 10th, 21 on the 11th, 21 on the 12th), or 70 if you stay for the 13th. Many pilgrims collect a few spare.

Is Qurbani included in Hajj packages?

Usually not. Pilgrims on Hajj al-Tamattu' must offer a sacrifice. You can buy an official Adahi voucher in advance, and your group will arrange it on the 10th. Qurbani is not included in Sada-e-Rehmat's Hajj 2027 prices.
