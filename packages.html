Every journey

What's included.

  • Maktab A, Zone 1The closest category to the Jamarat.
  • Private busesAir-conditioned group coaches with built-in toilets.
  • Gypsum-walled Mina tentsSofa-cum-beds, pillows and blankets, with the group's own washrooms.
  • A/C marquees in ArafatSofa mattresses in Mina and Arafat for real rest.
  • Buffet mealsIn Mina and Arafat, and in Aziziya on full board.
  • 24-hour drinks & ice creamHot and cold drinks in Aziziya, Mina and Arafat.
  • Muzdalifah kitCarpet mats, sleeping bags and snack boxes.
  • Attached bathroomsIn every Aziziya room, close to Mina.
  • Haramain high-speed trainBetween Makkah and Madinah, in the direction of your route.
Good to know: terms and exclusions
  • Prices are per person and exclude airfare and Qurbani.
  • Aziziya rooms are shared five beds, gender-segregated, unless you add a private room.
  • Makkah and Madinah rooms are private at the occupancy you choose: quad, triple or double.
  • Dates follow the Saudi Hijri calendar and may move by one day with the moon sighting and flight schedule.
  • Group transport is by bus throughout the journey.
  • The Maysan Al Harithia upgrade is on half board where it applies.
  • Unused services are not refundable if a pilgrim chooses not to take them.
  • All dates are tentative and confirmed in writing at booking.
