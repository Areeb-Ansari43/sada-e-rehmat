Hajj 2027 from Pakistan.

Pakistan's first multi-year Hajj policy covers 2027 to 2030. For Hajj 2027, Saudi Arabia has allocated Pakistan 179,210 places, split between the government scheme and authorised private Hajj organisers.

In short

  • Quota for Hajj 2027: 179,210: 107,526 government and 71,684 private.
  • Registration with the Ministry of Religious Affairs was mandatory; it opened on 22 June 2026.
  • Your passport must be valid until at least 16 November 2027.
  • Pay for private packages only through the official Hajj portal or app.

Private packages give you more choice: Maktab A tents in Zone 1, closer hotels, shorter or longer stays and Madinah before or after Hajj. Our Hajj 2027 collections are built on Maktab A.

Key dates and rules

  • Registration opened 22 June 2026 and was required before applying
  • Passports must be valid until 16 November 2027
  • The ministry set 20 November 2026 as the deadline to select and pay for a private package
  • Unused private quota can be reallocated by the ministry
  • Payments for private packages go through the official Hajj portal or app
Check before you pay

Deadlines can be extended or changed. Confirm every date on the Ministry of Religious Affairs website or the official Pak Hajj app before you make a payment.

Choosing a private package

Compare packages on the things that change your experience most: the Maktab category and zone in Mina (closer to the Jamarat means less walking), whether you stay in Aziziya or a Makkah hotel before Hajj, the hotels by name, the meal plan, room sharing, whether Madinah is before or after Hajj, and what is excluded (often airfare and Qurbani). Our package comparison guide has a full checklist.

Questions

What is the Hajj quota for Pakistan in 2027?

179,210 places: 107,526 for the government scheme and 71,684 for private Hajj organisers.

Is airfare included in private Hajj packages?

It depends on the package. Many private packages, including ours, are priced without airfare, so you can choose your flights.

Can I go for Hajj 2027 without registering?

No. The Ministry of Religious Affairs made registration mandatory for Hajj 2027 applications.
