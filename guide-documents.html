Your documents, in order.

Most travel problems on pilgrimage start with paperwork. Gather everything early, check names match exactly, and keep copies separate from the originals.

In short

  • Passport valid for 6+ months (for Pakistan's Hajj 2027 scheme: valid until 16 Nov 2027).
  • The name on your visa, tickets and bookings must match your passport exactly.
  • Keep digital copies in your email and paper copies apart from the originals.

The checklist

  • Passport with 6+ months validity and two blank pages
  • Visa (eVisa, Umrah visa or Hajj visa)
  • Nusuk account, Umrah or Hajj permit, and Rawdah permit
  • Meningitis ACWY certificate (and polio if travelling from Pakistan)
  • Flight tickets and hotel confirmations
  • Travel insurance documents
  • Emergency contact card in English and Arabic
  • Your operator's 24/7 contact in Saudi Arabia
  • For women under 18 or children: any guardian consent your route requires

Travelling with children

Each child needs their own passport, visa, vaccine certificate and Nusuk permit. Children under one are exempt from the ACWY requirement. Carry birth certificates if surnames differ from a parent's.

Keeping documents safe

  • Use a neck pouch or Ihram belt for your passport and permit on travel days.
  • Leave your passport in the hotel safe in Makkah and Madinah unless you need it; carry a copy and your hotel card.
  • Only send passport scans to your operator through a channel they've told you to use.

Questions

How much passport validity do I need for Umrah?

At least six months from your date of arrival in Saudi Arabia.

Do I need to print my Nusuk permit?

No, it's shown in the app, but a screenshot helps when signal is weak.
