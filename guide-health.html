Health & wellbeing

Health, heat & vaccinations: prepare your body for the journey

Pilgrimage can involve heat, crowds, long walks, altered sleep, and a demanding daily rhythm. A little preparation before departure can make it easier to stay well and ask for help early.

Check current requirements early

UK government pilgrimage advice says travellers must have a valid certificate of vaccination against ACWY strains of meningitis 10 days before arriving for Hajj and Umrah. Requirements can change, so check current official guidance and speak to a qualified travel-health professional before booking or departure.

Medication and medical information

Carry essential medication in original packaging and keep it in your hand luggage where appropriate. Bring a written list of medicines, doses, allergies, and emergency contacts. Check the rules for bringing medication into Saudi Arabia and ask your clinician about what to do if travel disrupts your routine.

Heat, hydration, and rest

Drink regularly, use shade where possible, wear suitable clothing, and avoid treating exhaustion as a test of willpower. Plan rest around the group itinerary and tell your companions if you feel dizzy, unusually weak, confused, or unwell. Get help early.

Walking and mobility

Train gradually with the footwear you will use. If you have mobility needs, ask about room location, transport, walking distances, accessibility, and how the itinerary can be adapted. A clear conversation before booking is better than trying to solve every issue after arrival.

A responsible health plan

Book any recommended appointments, check insurance, share key information with a trusted companion, and keep the local emergency and group contacts available offline. This guide is not medical advice; a qualified clinician should confirm anything personal or consequential.

Important: Requirements, visa routes, health rules, prices, and official booking arrangements can change. Check current official guidance for your nationality and dates before you travel.
