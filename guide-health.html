Health, heat and vaccinations.

Millions of people, intense heat and long days on foot make pilgrimage physically demanding. A few weeks of preparation prevents most of the problems pilgrims run into.

In short

  • Saudi Arabia requires a meningitis ACWY certificate issued at least 10 days before arrival.
  • Travellers from Pakistan should also carry proof of polio vaccination.
  • Heat is the biggest danger. Drink before you're thirsty and rest in the shade.
  • If you feel unwell with meningitis symptoms within two weeks of returning, call your GP or NHS 111 straight away.

In the UK, the ACWY vaccine for travel is usually given at a private travel clinic or pharmacy, not free on the NHS. Book six to eight weeks before you travel.

Heat and hydration

  • Drink water and Zamzam regularly, add oral rehydration salts on long days
  • Use an umbrella; men in Ihram cannot cover their heads, but shade is allowed
  • Walk in the early morning and at night where possible
  • Know the warning signs: headache, dizziness, confusion, no sweating, fainting. Get to shade and medical help
  • Saudi first-aid points and hospitals in Mina and Arafat treat pilgrims free of charge

Medicines and long-term conditions

Bring enough medicine for the whole trip plus a week, in original packaging, with a copy of your prescription. Some medicines are controlled in Saudi Arabia, including some strong painkillers and ADHD medicines, so check with the Saudi embassy before travelling. If you have diabetes, heart or kidney disease, see your GP before booking; fasting, heat and walking all affect your condition.

Get fit before you go

Expect to walk 10–15 km on many days and more during Hajj. Start walking daily six to eight weeks before you travel, in the footwear you'll wear.

After you return

Watch for meningitis symptoms

UKHSA advises anyone who becomes unwell with symptoms of meningitis (fever, severe headache, stiff neck, rash, confusion) within two weeks of returning from Saudi Arabia to contact their GP or NHS 111 immediately, and say where they have travelled.

A cough that lasts weeks after Hajj is common. See a doctor if it gets worse or you have a fever.

Questions

Is the meningitis vaccine compulsory for Umrah?

Yes. Saudi Arabia requires a valid meningitis ACWY certificate for Hajj and Umrah, issued at least 10 days before arrival.

How long is the ACWY vaccine valid for?

Conjugate ACWY vaccines are accepted for five years and polysaccharide vaccines for three, provided the dose was given at least 10 days before arrival.

Is the meningitis vaccine free on the NHS for Hajj?

Generally not. Travel ACWY vaccination is usually provided privately by travel clinics and pharmacies.
