Umrah and Hajj for women.

Women make up almost half of all pilgrims. The rites are the same, with a few differences in dress and practice, and some practical planning makes a real difference.

In short

  • Saudi Arabia no longer requires a mahram for women performing Umrah. Hajj routes may still set conditions.
  • Women's Ihram is ordinary modest clothing. No niqab or gloves.
  • A woman on her period can do every rite except Tawaf, and is excused from the farewell Tawaf.
  • Women do not jog in Tawaf or Sa'i, and trim a fingertip's length of hair.

Do I need a mahram?

Saudi Arabia removed the mahram requirement for women travelling for Umrah, and women can now apply for visas independently. For Hajj, rules depend on your route: national Hajj authorities and Nusuk Hajj may require women without a mahram to travel in an organised group. Separately, many scholars hold that a mahram is still required religiously, while others permit travel in a safe, trusted group. Discuss it with a scholar you trust.

Ihram for women

  • Wear loose, modest clothing in any colour. Comfortable abayas with deep pockets are popular.
  • Keep the face and hands uncovered in Ihram. Many scholars allow lowering a cloth over the face, without a fitted niqab, when near non-mahram men.
  • Normal shoes, socks and underwear are fine.
  • Avoid scented products after making the intention.

If your period starts

Menstruation does not stop you from entering Ihram or doing most rites. The Prophet ﷺ told Aisha (may Allah be pleased with her), when her period began during Hajj: “Do everything the pilgrim does, except Tawaf around the House until you are pure.” (Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī)

  • You can enter Ihram, stay at Arafat and Muzdalifah, stone the Jamarat and make duʿā' and dhikr.
  • Wait to perform Tawaf (and, for Umrah, the Sa'i that follows it) until your period ends and you have bathed.
  • The farewell Tawaf is waived for a woman on her period.
  • Some women take medication to delay their period; speak to your GP well in advance.
Short trips and Tawaf al-Ifadah

If your flight home is fixed and your period continues past it, this is a matter to discuss with a scholar before you travel. Arranging a little flexibility in your return date helps.

Practical tips

  • Women's prayer areas are separate. Agree a meeting point with your group by gate number.
  • Rawdah visits for women have their own time slots, booked on Nusuk.
  • Carry a small bag that fits under an abaya for your phone, permit and water.
  • Pack sanitary products and pain relief in your hand luggage.
  • Upper floors of the Haram are often calmer for Tawaf, though the circuit is longer.

Questions

Can a woman go for Umrah alone?

Under current Saudi rules, yes. Women can obtain an Umrah visa without a mahram. Many choose to travel with a trusted group, and scholars differ on the religious ruling.

Can a woman perform Sa'i during her period?

Most scholars say Sa'i does not require purity, but the Sa'i of Umrah must follow a valid Tawaf, so in practice she waits until she can perform Tawaf.
