Avoiding Hajj and Umrah scams.

Every year, pilgrims lose savings to fake Hajj places and vanishing operators. A few checks before you pay protect both your money and your Hajj.

In short

  • UK residents: Hajj places are sold only on Nusuk Hajj.
  • Pakistan: pay for private Hajj packages only through the official ministry portal or app.
  • Never travel for Hajj on a tourist or visit visa.
  • UK Umrah packages that include flights should be ATOL protected.

Warning signs

  • A Hajj place offered outside Nusuk or your national scheme
  • Prices far below everyone else's
  • Pressure to pay today, in cash or to a personal account
  • No written terms, hotel names or company details
  • No physical office or registered company
  • “Hajj on a visit visa” or “no permit needed”

How to check an operator

  • UK: look up the company on Companies House, check ATOL protection on the CAA website for flight packages, and look for ABTA membership.
  • Pakistan: check the organiser is on the Ministry of Religious Affairs' list of authorised Hajj organisers.
  • Ask for everything in writing: hotels, dates, room sharing, inclusions, exclusions and refund terms.
  • Pay by card or bank transfer to a company account, never a personal one.

Where to report fraud

  • UK: Action Fraud, 0300 123 2040 or actionfraud.police.uk
  • Pakistan: the Ministry of Religious Affairs complaint cell and the FIA Cyber Crime Wing

Questions

Can I perform Hajj on a visit visa?

No. Saudi Arabia only allows Hajj with an official Hajj permit, and enforces it strictly with fines, deportation and entry bans.

What is ATOL protection?

ATOL is a UK scheme run by the Civil Aviation Authority that protects you if a travel company selling a flight-inclusive package fails.
