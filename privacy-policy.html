Privacy & your data

This working draft explains how Sada-e-Rehmat Ltd handles personal information when you browse this website, contact us, or ask us to plan Hajj, Umrah, Ziyarat, travel, accommodation, transport, or related services.

Who we are

Sada-e-Rehmat Ltd is a UK private limited company registered in England and Wales under company number 16043621. Our registered office is 685 Staines Road, Feltham, United Kingdom, TW14 8PA. For privacy questions, contact info@sada-e-rehmat.com.

Information we may collect

Depending on the service you request, this may include your name, contact details, address, passport or identity-document details, nationality, date of birth, travel dates, visa information, emergency contact details, accessibility or dietary requirements, payment or booking information, and messages you send to us. Please only share information needed for your enquiry or booking.

Why we use it

We use information to respond to enquiries, provide quotations, arrange requested travel services, support visa and booking processes, communicate important journey information, administer our relationship with you, protect our website and business, and meet legal or regulatory obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Lawful bases

Depending on the context, we may rely on steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, compliance with a legal obligation, legitimate interests such as operating and securing our service, or consent where consent is required. If you withdraw consent, this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

Who we may share it with

Where necessary to deliver your requested journey, information may be shared with relevant visa or travel partners, airlines, accommodation providers, transport operators, guides, technology and form providers, professional advisers, or authorities. We aim to share only what is needed and expect service providers to handle information securely.

International transfers and pilgrimage documents

Hajj and Umrah arrangements can involve services in Saudi Arabia and other countries. Where personal information leaves the UK, we will consider the applicable transfer safeguards and the requirements of UK data-protection law. Keep your passport and travel documents secure and do not send copies by unsecured channels unless our team tells you how to do so.

How long we keep information

We keep information only for as long as needed for the purpose collected, to complete or evidence a booking, to meet legal, accounting, insurance, complaint-handling, or dispute-resolution requirements, and to protect our legitimate interests. Retention periods depend on the type of record.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to certain processing. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office. Contact us at info@sada-e-rehmat.com to exercise a right or ask a question.

Security and updates

We use reasonable organisational and technical measures, but no internet transmission is completely risk-free. We may update this notice when our services, technology, or legal requirements change.
