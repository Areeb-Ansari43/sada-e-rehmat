Ziyarat in Makkah.

A Ziyarat tour is a chance to stand where the story of Islam began. None of these visits is part of the rites, so plan them around your prayers in the Haram, not instead of them.

In short

  • Most tours take three to four hours by coach, early morning.
  • The climb to the Cave of Hira takes about an hour each way and is steep.
  • Masjid Aisha (Tan'eem) is where pilgrims in Makkah enter Ihram for another Umrah.

Etiquette

Scholars caution against rituals at these sites that have no basis in the Sunnah, such as rubbing walls, tying cloth or praying towards the caves. Visit to learn and reflect, send salam on the Prophet ﷺ and pray for the believers.

Questions

Is Ziyarat part of Umrah?

No. Umrah is Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i and cutting the hair. Ziyarat visits are optional.

Can I do another Umrah from Makkah?

Yes. Go to Masjid Aisha at Tan'eem (or another point outside the Haram boundary), enter Ihram there and book a new permit on Nusuk.
