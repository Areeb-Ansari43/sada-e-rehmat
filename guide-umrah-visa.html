Umrah visas, explained.

Getting into Saudi Arabia and being allowed into the Haram for Umrah are two separate things. You need a visa that permits Umrah, and a free Umrah permit from the Nusuk app.

In short

  • British passport: the Saudi tourist eVisa allows Umrah outside the Hajj season. It is usually issued online within a few days.
  • Pakistani passport: you normally need an Umrah visa through an approved Umrah company, unless you hold a valid UK, US or Schengen visa.
  • Every pilgrim also needs a Nusuk Umrah permit with a time slot. It is free.
  • Tourist and Umrah visas cannot be used to perform Hajj.

Rules change each season

Saudi Arabia updates visa products often. In July 2026 it announced a 90-day, multiple-entry Umrah visa valid for a year, linked to buying a service package from an approved Nusuk provider. Always check the official portal before you book flights.

The Nusuk permit

Nusuk is the official Saudi platform for Hajj and Umrah. Once you have a visa, download the app, register with your passport and visa details, and book a date and time for your Umrah. The permit is free. Show it when asked at the entrances to the Mataf. You also use Nusuk to book the Rawdah in Madinah (see our Madinah guide).

Umrah season dates

Umrah visas are not issued for travel during Hajj. For the 1448 AH season, visa issuance reportedly resumed on 31 May 2026, with 9 March 2027 reported as the last date for issuing an Umrah visa before Hajj 2027. Plan to complete Umrah and leave well before the Hajj period begins.

What you will need

  • Passport valid for at least six months from arrival
  • A recent passport-style photo (digital)
  • Meningitis ACWY certificate, issued at least 10 days before arrival
  • Confirmed accommodation and return or onward ticket
  • Health insurance (included in the eVisa and Umrah visa fee)
  • Polio vaccination evidence if you are travelling from Pakistan

Questions

Can I do Umrah on a tourist visa?

Yes. Holders of the Saudi tourist eVisa can perform Umrah outside the Hajj season, as long as they book a permit on Nusuk.

How long does an Umrah visa take?

The eVisa is often issued within 24–72 hours. Umrah visas through providers usually take a few working days; allow longer before Ramadan.

Can women get an Umrah visa without a mahram?

Yes. Saudi Arabia no longer requires women to travel with a mahram for Umrah. See our guide for women.
