About Sada-e-Rehmat

The voice of mercy.

A Hajj and Umrah specialist with offices in Feltham and Rawalpindi, and twelve years of pilgrims behind us.

We began with a simple conviction: the journey of a lifetime deserves to be organised with care, clarity and devotion. A small team of guides has grown into a full pilgrimage service trusted by thousands of families.
