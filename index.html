Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before your sacred journey — answered with clarity.

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah?+

Hajj is the annual pilgrimage with specific dates and rites, while Umrah is a shorter pilgrimage that can be performed throughout the year.

When is the best time to perform Umrah?+

Umrah can be performed year-round. Our team can help you choose dates that balance weather, crowds, and your travel preferences.

Do you handle the entire visa process?+

Yes. Our specialists guide you through the documentation, application, and approval process from start to finish.

How early should I book my Hajj package?+

We recommend registering as early as possible because availability, visas, and accommodation options are limited.

Are accommodations within walking distance of the Haram?+

Package options clearly explain hotel locations and transport arrangements so you can choose the comfort level that suits you.

What is included in your packages?+

Depending on the package, inclusions may cover visa support, flights, hotels, ground transport, guidance, and on-ground assistance.

Do you offer group or family packages?+

Yes. We can arrange family, private, and group journeys with flexible accommodation and travel support.

What support do you provide during the journey?+

Our on-ground team remains available throughout your pilgrimage for coordination, guidance, and practical assistance.
