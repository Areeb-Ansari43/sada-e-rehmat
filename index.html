Questions

Asked, and answered.

What is the difference between Hajj and Umrah?

Hajj is the annual pilgrimage with fixed dates in Dhul Hijjah and is obligatory once for those able. Umrah is shorter and can be performed at any time of the year.

What does Maktab A, Zone 1 mean?

Maktabs are the official service categories in Mina and Arafat. Category A is the highest standard, and Zone 1 is the closest area to the Jamarat, which means shorter walks on the days of stoning.

Are flights included?

No. Every Hajj 2027 price is per person and excludes airfare and Qurbani, so you can fly from the UK or Pakistan on the airline that suits you. Our team can advise on flights that fit your arrival date.

What is the difference between Madinah first and Makkah first?

Madinah first means you visit the Prophet's Mosque before Hajj and fly home from Jeddah. Makkah first means you begin in Makkah, perform Hajj, then travel to Madinah by the Haramain train and fly home from Madinah.

Is the Aziziya room shared?

Yes, by default. Aziziya rooms have five beds, are gender-segregated and have attached bathrooms. You can add a private quad, triple or double room for an extra charge.

Can the dates change?

The itinerary follows the Saudi Hijri calendar, so dates may move by one day with the moon sighting and flight schedules. We confirm everything in writing at booking.

Do you arrange Umrah too?

Yes, year-round, including Ramadan. Umrah journeys are shaped around your dates, hotel preference and group size, so we quote each one individually.

Places in Maktab A are limited each season. Tell us your dates and who is travelling, and we will hold the right journey for you.
