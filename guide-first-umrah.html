How to perform Umrah, step by step.

Umrah has four essential acts: Ihram, Tawaf, Sa'i and cutting the hair. Most pilgrims complete it in three to five hours. Here is the whole thing, in order, with the duas and the mistakes to avoid.

In short

  • Umrah = Ihram → Tawaf → Sa'i → shave or trim. That's it.
  • Make your intention before you pass the Miqat, which on most flights happens in the air.
  • Book your Umrah time slot on the Nusuk app before you go to the Haram.
  • Allow 3–5 hours, more in Ramadan. Go after Isha or before Fajr for lighter crowds.

Step by step

لَبَّيْكَIntention · Niyyah
تَقَبَّلَ اللَّهُMay Allah accept it
01/07
Step 1

Prepare and enter Ihram

Take a bath (ghusl), trim nails and remove unwanted hair. Men may put fragrance on the body, not the Ihram cloth. Men wear two unstitched white sheets; women wear normal modest clothing. Then, before the Miqat, make the intention: Labbayk Allāhumma ʿumrah.

GhuslTwo white sheets for menIntention before the Miqat
Step 2

Recite the Talbiyah

From the moment you are in Ihram, repeat the Talbiyah often: men aloud, women quietly. Stop when you begin Tawaf. Keep the Ihram rules: no perfume, no cutting hair or nails, no arguments.

Men aloud, women softlyStop at the start of Tawaf
Step 3

Seven circuits of Tawaf

Enter with your right foot. Start at the line facing the Black Stone, raise your right hand and say Allāhu akbar. Walk seven circuits anticlockwise, keeping the Kaaba on your left. Men uncover the right shoulder (idṭibāʿ) for this Tawaf and walk briskly in the first three circuits if space allows.

7 circuitsAnticlockwiseStart and end at the Black Stone
Step 4

Pray two rak'ahs

Cover the right shoulder again. Pray two short rak'ahs behind Maqam Ibrahim if you can, or anywhere in the mosque if it is crowded. It is sunnah to recite Surah al-Kāfirūn in the first and al-Ikhlāṣ in the second.

2 rak'ahsAnywhere if crowded
Step 5

Drink Zamzam water

Drink your fill of Zamzam, facing the Qibla if you can, and make duʿā'. The Prophet ﷺ said Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for. Coolers are placed throughout the mosque.

Any cooler in the HaramMake duʿā'
Step 6

Seven laps of Sa'i

Climb Safa, face the Kaaba and praise Allah. Walk to Marwa: that is one lap. Marwa back to Safa is the second. You finish on Marwa after the seventh. Men jog lightly between the green lights. Wudu is recommended but not required for Sa'i.

7 lapsStart Safa, end MarwaAbout 3.2 km
Step 7

Shave or trim the hair

Men shave the head (best) or trim evenly all over. Women gather their hair and cut a fingertip's length from the ends. Your Umrah is complete and the Ihram restrictions are lifted.

Men: shave or trimWomen: a fingertip's length

Before you travel

Umrah can be performed at any time of year except the days of Hajj. Before you fly, you need a visa that allows Umrah (the Saudi tourist eVisa or an Umrah visa), a valid meningitis ACWY certificate issued at least 10 days before arrival, and an Umrah permit booked through the official Nusuk app. Read our Umrah visa guide and health guide for the details.

  • Passport valid for at least six months
  • Visa that permits Umrah
  • ACWY certificate (10+ days before arrival)
  • Nusuk account and Umrah permit
  • Ihram clothing in your hand luggage
  • Unscented soap, deodorant and lip balm

Ihram and the Miqat

The Miqat is the boundary you must not cross without being in Ihram. If you are flying straight to Jeddah from the UK or Pakistan, you will cross it in the air, usually 20 to 40 minutes before landing. Jeddah itself is inside the boundary, so you cannot wait to change at the airport.

The simplest approach: shower and change into Ihram at home or the departure airport, then make the intention when the crew announce the Miqat, or a little before. Our Miqat guide explains each route, including what to do if you are going to Madinah first.

The Talbiyah · from the moment you enter Ihram
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, inna l-ḥamda wa n-niʿmata laka wa l-mulk, lā sharīka lak.
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty. You have no partner.
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim

Tawaf, done properly

Entering the mosque · right foot first
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā rasūli-llāh. Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · Sunan Ibn Mājah

Tawaf starts and ends in line with the Black Stone, marked by a green light on the wall opposite. Point your right hand towards it and say Allāhu akbar at the start of each circuit; do not push to kiss it. There is no fixed duʿā' for each circuit, so pray in any language from the heart. Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, it is sunnah to recite:

Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi d-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fi l-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ʿadhāba n-nār.
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur'an 2:201 · Sunan Abī Dāwūd

If you lose count, go with the lower number. If the prayer starts during Tawaf, join the congregation and continue from where you stopped. You need wudu for Tawaf.

Sa'i between Safa and Marwa

As you approach Safa, recite Inna ṣ-ṣafā wa l-marwata min shaʿā'iri-llāh (Qur'an 2:158) and say, “I begin with what Allah began with.” Then face the Kaaba and say:

On Safa and on Marwa, facing the Kaaba
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Allāhu akbar (×3). Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamdu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr. Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdah, anjaza waʿdah, wa naṣara ʿabdah, wa hazama l-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Allah is the Greatest. There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. There is no god but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant and defeated the confederates alone.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (the Prophet's ﷺ Hajj, narrated by Jābir). Repeat three times, making your own duʿā' in between.

The full Sa'i is about 3.2 km on smooth marble. The upper floors and the roof are usually less crowded, and electric carts and wheelchairs are available for elderly pilgrims.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Crossing the Miqat without Ihram, then changing in Jeddah
  • Starting Tawaf away from the Black Stone line
  • Keeping the right shoulder uncovered outside Tawaf, including in prayer
  • Counting Safa → Marwa → Safa as one lap (it's two)
  • Cutting only a few strands of hair
  • Taking selfies or blocking the flow in the Mataf
A note on differences of opinion

The schools of Islamic law differ on some details, such as how much hair must be trimmed or the penalty for a missed act. If you follow a particular madhab, check these points with a scholar you trust before travelling.

Questions

How long does Umrah take?

Tawaf usually takes 45–90 minutes and Sa'i 45–75 minutes, depending on crowds and your pace. Allow three to five hours in total, longer in Ramadan and on Fridays.

Can I perform Umrah more than once on a trip?

Yes. Pilgrims staying in Makkah exit to the nearest boundary, usually Masjid Aisha at Tan'eem, enter Ihram there and perform another Umrah. Each needs its own Nusuk permit.

Do I need wudu for Sa'i?

It is recommended but not required according to most scholars. Wudu is required for Tawaf.

Do I need a Nusuk permit for Umrah?

Yes. Since 2022 every pilgrim needs an Umrah permit with a time slot from the Nusuk app. The permit is free.
