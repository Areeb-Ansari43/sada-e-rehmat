Two packages at a similar price can feel completely different on the ground. These are the questions that reveal what you're actually buying.

In short

  • The Maktab category and zone in Mina decides how far you walk during Hajj.
  • Ask for hotel names, not “5-star or similar”.
  • Check what's excluded: airfare and Qurbani are common exclusions.

12 questions to ask

  1. Which Maktab category and zone in Mina? Category A in Zone 1 is closest to the Jamarat.
  2. What kind of tents? Gypsum-walled, air-conditioned tents with sofa-beds are far more comfortable than basic tents.
  3. Where do I stay before Hajj? Aziziya (near Mina) or a hotel by the Haram, and for how many nights?
  4. Which hotels exactly? Names, distance to the Haram, and whether the hotel is confirmed.
  5. How many people per room? Quad, triple, double, and whether Aziziya rooms are shared.
  6. Which meals? Full board, half board or breakfast only, in each city.
  7. Madinah before or after Hajj? And how do you travel between cities: coach or Haramain train?
  8. What's excluded? Airfare, Qurbani, visa fees, Ziyarat, insurance.
  9. Who supports us on the ground? Named staff, phone numbers, scholar in the group.
  10. What are the dates in Gregorian and Hijri? And what happens if they move by a day?
  11. What are the payment and cancellation terms? In writing.
  12. Is the operator authorised? Nusuk-approved, ministry-licensed, or ATOL protected for UK flights.

Questions

What does Maktab A mean?

Maktabs are the service categories in Mina and Arafat. Category A is the highest standard and is located in the zones closest to the Jamarat.

Is Aziziya a good place to stay?

Aziziya is a district between Makkah and Mina. It's convenient for the days of Hajj, but further from the Haram than hotels in the central area.
