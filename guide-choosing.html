Choosing well

How to compare pilgrimage packages with confidence

A lower headline price does not always mean better value. The right comparison looks at what is included, how clearly it is confirmed, and how well the package fits the needs of your group.

Start with the total journey

Write down departure city, travel dates, number of travellers, room occupancy, mobility needs, preferred hotel distance, and whether you want guided Ziyarat or a quieter itinerary. A package that is perfect for one family may be wrong for another.

Compare what is actually included

Ask whether the written price includes flights, visa support, hotel names, room type, meals, airport transfers, intercity transport, Ziyarat, group leadership, and support in Saudi Arabia. If something is described as “near” or “premium”, ask for a practical explanation.

Read the terms before paying

Keep a copy of the quotation and terms. Check deposit amounts, payment dates, amendment fees, cancellation rules, what happens if a flight or visa changes, and which company is responsible for each part of the arrangement. Ask for important answers in writing.

Travel protection and trust

Research the operator and check any protection or licensing that applies to your booking. UK government advice warns travellers about Hajj and Umrah scams and recommends keeping payment records, getting written confirmations, and being cautious about offers that appear too good to be true.

A comparison scorecard

  • Clarity of inclusions: 1–5.
  • Hotel suitability and distance: 1–5.
  • Support and communication: 1–5.
  • Flexibility for your group: 1–5.
  • Written terms and payment trail: 1–5.

Important: Requirements, visa routes, health rules, prices and official booking arrangements can change. Check current official guidance for your nationality and dates before you travel.
