Cookie Policy

This working draft explains how Sada-e-Rehmat Ltd uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies on this website.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on a device. Similar technologies can also store or access information on a device. They may help a website remember preferences, keep a service secure, or understand how a website is used.

What this website uses

Our static website uses essential browser storage to remember whether you accepted or declined the cookie notice. This supports the site’s preference experience. We do not intentionally set advertising cookies or build advertising profiles through this site.

Embedded services

The Contact page may load Google Maps. Google may process technical information through its own services when an embedded map is requested. Review Google’s own privacy and cookie information before using embedded map features.

Consent and your choices

We show a clear cookie notice and ask for an active choice. Essential technologies may be used where strictly necessary for the service you request. Non-essential cookies or similar technologies should not be set unless the required consent has been obtained. You can decline through the banner, clear your browser storage, or adjust your browser settings. Clearing storage may cause the banner to appear again.

How to ask questions

For questions about our use of cookies, contact info@sada-e-rehmat.com. This notice may be updated if the website or its embedded services change.
