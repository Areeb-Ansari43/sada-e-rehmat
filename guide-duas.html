Duas for Umrah and Hajj.

There are only a handful of fixed words in Umrah and Hajj. Everything else is your own duʿā', in any language. Here are the ones that come from the Sunnah, in the order you will need them.

In short

  • There is no fixed duʿā' for each circuit of Tawaf. Booklets that assign one are not from the Sunnah.
  • Learn the Talbiyah and the Safa dhikr by heart before you travel.
  • Write your personal duʿā' list at home, for Arafat, Multazam and Zamzam.

Entering Ihram

The Talbiyah · from the moment you enter Ihram
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ لَبَّيْكَ، لَبَّيْكَ لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ لَبَّيْكَ، إِنَّ الْحَمْدَ وَالنِّعْمَةَ لَكَ وَالْمُلْكَ، لَا شَرِيكَ لَكَ
Labbayk Allāhumma labbayk, labbayka lā sharīka laka labbayk, inna l-ḥamda wa n-niʿmata laka wa l-mulk, lā sharīka lak.
Here I am, O Allah, here I am. Here I am, You have no partner, here I am. All praise and blessings are Yours, and all sovereignty. You have no partner.
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī and Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
Intention for Umrah
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ عُمْرَةً
Labbayk Allāhumma ʿumrah.
Here I am, O Allah, for Umrah.
Intention for Hajj (on 8 Dhul Hijjah for Tamattu')
لَبَّيْكَ اللَّهُمَّ حَجًّا
Labbayk Allāhumma ḥajjan.
Here I am, O Allah, for Hajj.

At the Haram and in Tawaf

Entering the mosque · right foot first
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ، وَالصَّلَاةُ وَالسَّلَامُ عَلَىٰ رَسُولِ اللَّهِ، اللَّهُمَّ افْتَحْ لِي أَبْوَابَ رَحْمَتِكَ
Bismillāh, wa ṣ-ṣalātu wa s-salāmu ʿalā rasūli-llāh. Allāhumma-ftaḥ lī abwāba raḥmatik.
In the name of Allah, and peace and blessings upon the Messenger of Allah. O Allah, open for me the doors of Your mercy.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim · Sunan Ibn Mājah
Facing the Black Stone at the start of each circuit
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar.
Allah is the Greatest.
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī. Many add “Bismillāh” before it, as Ibn ʿUmar did.
Between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone
رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ
Rabbanā ātinā fi d-dunyā ḥasanatan wa fi l-ākhirati ḥasanatan wa qinā ʿadhāba n-nār.
Our Lord, give us good in this world and good in the Hereafter, and protect us from the punishment of the Fire.
Qur'an 2:201 · Sunan Abī Dāwūd
Approaching Maqam Ibrahim
وَاتَّخِذُوا مِن مَّقَامِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ مُصَلًّى
Wa-ttakhidhū min maqāmi Ibrāhīma muṣallā.
And take the station of Ibrahim as a place of prayer.
Qur'an 2:125 · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim

Drinking Zamzam

The Prophet ﷺ said: “The water of Zamzam is for whatever it is drunk for.” (Sunan Ibn Mājah). Drink it facing the Qibla, in three sips, and ask Allah for whatever you need. A duʿā' reported from Ibn ʿAbbās is:

Drinking Zamzam (reported from Ibn ʿAbbās)
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَسْأَلُكَ عِلْمًا نَافِعًا، وَرِزْقًا وَاسِعًا، وَشِفَاءً مِنْ كُلِّ دَاءٍ
Allāhumma innī as'aluka ʿilman nāfiʿā, wa rizqan wāsiʿā, wa shifā'an min kulli dā'.
O Allah, I ask You for beneficial knowledge, abundant provision and healing from every illness.
Sunan ad-Dāraquṭnī

On Safa and Marwa

Approaching Safa
إِنَّ الصَّفَا وَالْمَرْوَةَ مِن شَعَائِرِ اللَّهِ · أَبْدَأُ بِمَا بَدَأَ اللَّهُ بِهِ
Inna ṣ-ṣafā wa l-marwata min shaʿā'iri-llāh. Abda'u bimā bada'a-llāhu bih.
Indeed Safa and Marwa are among the symbols of Allah. I begin with what Allah began with.
Qur'an 2:158 · Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
On Safa and on Marwa, facing the Kaaba
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ. لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ، أَنْجَزَ وَعْدَهُ، وَنَصَرَ عَبْدَهُ، وَهَزَمَ الْأَحْزَابَ وَحْدَهُ
Allāhu akbar (×3). Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamdu wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr. Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdah, anjaza waʿdah, wa naṣara ʿabdah, wa hazama l-aḥzāba waḥdah.
Allah is the Greatest. There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things. There is no god but Allah alone. He fulfilled His promise, helped His servant and defeated the confederates alone.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim (the Prophet's ﷺ Hajj, narrated by Jābir). Repeat three times, making your own duʿā' in between.

During Hajj

The best duʿā' · the Day of Arafah
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ، وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
Lā ilāha illa-llāhu waḥdahū lā sharīka lah, lahu l-mulku wa lahu l-ḥamd, wa huwa ʿalā kulli shay'in qadīr.
There is no god but Allah alone, without partner. His is the dominion and His is the praise, and He has power over all things.
Jāmiʿ at-Tirmidhī: “The best duʿā' is the duʿā' of the Day of Arafah.”
At Mash'ar al-Haram, Muzdalifah, after Fajr
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ، لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ
Allāhu akbar, lā ilāha illa-llāh.
Allah is the Greatest; there is no god but Allah. The Prophet ﷺ faced the Qibla and made duʿā', takbīr and tahlīl until it was very light.
Ṣaḥīḥ Muslim
With each pebble at the Jamarat
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allāhu akbar.
Allah is the Greatest.
Ṣaḥīḥ al-Bukhārī

Visiting the Prophet ﷺ

At the grave of the Prophet ﷺ, then Abu Bakr and Umar
السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا رَسُولَ اللَّهِ · السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ · السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكَ يَا عُمَرُ
As-salāmu ʿalayka yā Rasūla-llāh. As-salāmu ʿalayka yā Abā Bakr. As-salāmu ʿalayka yā ʿUmar.
Peace be upon you, O Messenger of Allah. Peace be upon you, O Abu Bakr. Peace be upon you, O Umar.
Reported from Ibn ʿUmar

Then step away, face the Qibla and make your own duʿā'. See our Madinah guide for the Rawdah permit.

Questions

Is there a special dua for each circuit of Tawaf?

No. Apart from Allāhu akbar at the Black Stone and Rabbanā ātinā between the Yemeni Corner and the Black Stone, there is no fixed duʿā' for each circuit. Make your own duʿā' and dhikr, or recite Qur'an.

Can I make dua in English or Urdu?

Yes. Personal duʿā' can be made in any language. Only the Qur'an recitation in prayer must be in Arabic.
