Start with your passport

Check the passport used for travel, its expiry date, blank pages, and whether the name matches your other travel documents. Keep a secure digital copy and a paper copy stored separately from the original. Do not send passport scans through an unknown channel.

If a child or another family member is travelling, check each person separately rather than assuming one traveller’s documents cover the whole group.

What information may be requested

A travel operator may need names exactly as shown on passports, dates of birth, nationality, contact details, travel dates, accommodation preferences, and information needed for visa or booking work. Ask why a document is needed, who will receive it, and how it will be stored.

Hajj and Umrah are not the same booking journey

Hajj arrangements are seasonal and official routes can be specific to the traveller’s country and visa type. For UK travellers, current government guidance says Hajj places should be booked through the official Nusuk Hajj platform. Umrah routes can also change, so confirm the current Saudi process before relying on an older blog or social-media post.

Questions to ask before you pay

  • What exactly is included in the written price?
  • Which visa route is being used, and who is responsible for each step?
  • Which hotel names, room types, transport arrangements, and dates are confirmed?
  • What happens if a document is delayed or a requirement changes?
  • What are the cancellation, amendment, and refund terms?

A simple document folder

Create one folder for each traveller containing the passport copy, booking confirmations, insurance details, emergency contacts, medication list, and copies of important messages. Keep the original passport with you and leave a copy with someone you trust at home.
