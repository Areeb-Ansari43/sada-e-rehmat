Madinah, the city of the Prophet ﷺ.

Visiting Madinah is not part of Umrah or Hajj, but most pilgrims would not travel without it. Here is how to plan your time, book the Rawdah and visit the places of the Prophet's ﷺ life.

In short

  • You need a free Rawdah permit from the Nusuk app. Slots open in advance and go quickly.
  • Men and women have separate Rawdah times.
  • Praying two rak'ahs in Masjid Quba carries the reward of an Umrah (Sunan Ibn Mājah).
  • There is no Ihram for Madinah; it is outside the Miqat.

The Prophet's Mosque ﷺ

A prayer in Masjid an-Nabawi is worth more than a thousand prayers elsewhere, except Masjid al-Haram (Bukhari and Muslim). Enter with the right foot and the duʿā' for entering a mosque.

1,000× rewardOpen 24 hours

The Rawdah

The area between the Prophet's ﷺ house and his minbar is “a garden from the gardens of Paradise.” Book a slot on Nusuk, arrive early at the gate shown, and pray two rak'ahs calmly.

The first mosque in Islam

About 3.5 km south of the Prophet's Mosque. The Prophet ﷺ visited it every Saturday. Making wudu at your hotel and praying two rak'ahs in Quba carries the reward of an Umrah.

Reward of UmrahGo on a Saturday morning
04

The mountain that loves us

Site of the Battle of Uhud. Visit the graves of the martyrs, including Hamza (may Allah be pleased with him), and the archers' hill (Jabal al-Rumāt).

The cemetery of Madinah

Next to the Prophet's Mosque, where many of his family and Companions are buried. Usually open to men after Fajr and Asr. Greet the people of the graves and pray for them.

Booking the Rawdah on Nusuk

  1. Open the Nusuk app and log in with the account linked to your visa.
  2. Choose the Rawdah permit (men or women).
  3. Pick a date and time. Slots are released in advance and fill quickly; check regularly, and use the waitlist if offered.
  4. Save the permit and arrive at the stated gate 20–30 minutes early.

Permits are free. Children usually need their own permit, and very young children may not be admitted. Rules on how often you can book have changed in the past, so check the app for current limits.

Greeting the Prophet ﷺ

After praying, walk calmly along the front of the mosque past the Prophet's ﷺ grave. Say As-salāmu ʿalayka yā Rasūla-llāh, then step right and greet Abu Bakr, then Umar (may Allah be pleased with them). Keep your voice low, keep moving, and face the Qibla when you make duʿā'.

More places in Madinah

  • Masjid al-Qiblatain, where the Qibla changed from Jerusalem to Makkah.
  • The Seven Mosques (al-Masājid as-Sab'ah), near the site of the Battle of the Trench.
  • The date market, for Ajwa and other Madinah dates.
  • The Dar al-Madinah Museum, on the history of the city.

Questions

Do I need a permit for the Rawdah?

Yes. A free permit with a time slot is booked through the Nusuk app.

How many days should I spend in Madinah?

Three to five days is common. It gives time for the Rawdah, Quba, Uhud and plenty of prayers in the mosque. Our Hajj 2027 journeys include two to four nights.

Do I need Ihram in Madinah?

No. Madinah is outside the Miqat. You enter Ihram when you leave Madinah for Makkah, at Dhul Hulayfah.
