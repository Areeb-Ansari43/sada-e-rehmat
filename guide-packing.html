What to pack for Umrah and Hajj.

Pack light, pack for heat and crowds, and keep the essentials on you. Here's everything, grouped so you can tick it off.

In short

  • Carry documents, medicines and one set of Ihram in your hand luggage.
  • Everything for Ihram must be unscented.
  • For Hajj, pack a separate small backpack for the five days in Mina and Arafat.

Documents and money

  • Passport, plus photocopies and phone photos
  • Visa or Hajj permit, Nusuk app logged in
  • ACWY vaccine certificate
  • Booking confirmations and emergency contacts
  • Travel insurance details
  • Bank card and some Saudi riyals in cash
  • Pen for immigration and luggage tags

Clothing and Ihram

  • Men: 2 sets of Ihram, Ihram belt, safety pins
  • Women: 3–4 loose abayas, comfortable hijabs, socks
  • Light cotton clothes for after Ihram
  • A light jumper for air-conditioned hotels and coaches (and Madinah nights in winter)
  • Prayer mat (small, foldable)

Footwear

  • Sandals for Ihram that leave the ankles bare, worn in at home
  • Comfortable trainers for after Ihram
  • Shoe bag (you'll carry shoes in the Haram)
  • Blister plasters

Health and toiletries

  • Regular medicines in original packaging, with prescription copy
  • Paracetamol, antihistamine, rehydration salts, throat lozenges
  • Unscented soap, shampoo, deodorant, wet wipes
  • Vaseline or anti-chafe balm
  • SPF sunscreen (unscented if in Ihram), lip balm
  • Face masks for crowds
  • Small hand sanitiser

Extra for the days of Hajj

  • Small backpack or crossbody bag
  • Light umbrella for shade
  • Refillable water bottle and a spray bottle for cooling
  • Pebble pouch for the Jamarat
  • Phone power bank and short cable
  • Earplugs and an eye mask for Mina
  • Thin sleeping mat if not provided (our Muzdalifah kit includes mats and sleeping bags)

Leave at home

  • Large suitcases you can't lift alone, as lifts are crowded
  • Valuable jewellery
  • Drones and professional camera gear
  • Anything you'd be upset to lose in a crowd

Questions

Can I take Zamzam water home?

Yes. Most airlines allow one 5-litre sealed Zamzam bottle per passenger, bought at the airport or approved outlets. Check your airline's rules.

Should I take a suitcase or a backpack?

One medium suitcase plus a small backpack works best. You'll use the backpack every day in the Haram and during the days of Hajj.
