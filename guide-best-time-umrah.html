The best time for Umrah.

Umrah has no wrong month, but the experience changes a lot with the season. Here's how weather, crowds and price shift through the year, and the dates that matter for 2026–27.

In short

  • Coolest and calmest: late November to early February, outside school holidays.
  • Ramadan 2027 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid al-Fitr around 9–10 March. Umrah in Ramadan carries great reward, and the highest prices.
  • Umrah visas stop being issued before Hajj; 9 March 2027 was reported as the last issue date this season.
  • Summer (June–September) is very hot, but often cheapest.

Umrah in Ramadan

The Prophet ﷺ said that Umrah in Ramadan is equal to Hajj in reward (Bukhari and Muslim). It is also the busiest time of the year: book hotels and Nusuk permits early, expect Tawaf to take far longer, and plan around Taraweeh and Qiyam. In 2027, Ramadan overlaps the UK February half-term, which pushes prices up further.

Getting the timing right

  • Avoid Fridays and the first and last days of school holidays if you want quieter Tawaf
  • Perform Tawaf after Isha or between Fajr and sunrise for lighter crowds
  • Leave room for Madinah: three days or more
  • Book your Nusuk permit as soon as your visa is issued

Questions

When is the cheapest time for Umrah?

Usually the hot summer months, outside UK school holidays, and the weeks just after the Umrah season reopens after Hajj.

When is Ramadan 2027?

Ramadan 1448 is expected to begin around 8 February 2027, with Eid al-Fitr on about 9 or 10 March 2027, depending on moon sighting.
