Where is the Miqat on my flight?

Jeddah is inside the Miqat boundary, so pilgrims flying straight there must be in Ihram before landing. Here is how to get it right from the UK or Pakistan, and what changes if you go to Madinah first.

In short

  • You must be in Ihram before you cross the Miqat. Jeddah is inside it.
  • Flying to Jeddah: change at home or the airport, make the intention when the crew announce the Miqat, or a little before.
  • Flying to Madinah first: no Ihram on the flight. Enter Ihram at Dhul Hulayfah before travelling to Makkah.
  • On the Haramain train from Madinah, be in Ihram before boarding.

The five Miqat points

The Prophet ﷺ set five boundaries around the Haram. Anyone intending Umrah or Hajj must enter Ihram at, or before, the one they pass or come level with.

Flying from the UK

Flights from London, Manchester or Birmingham to Jeddah usually approach from the north or north-west and pass the Miqat line roughly 20–40 minutes before landing. Your exact path depends on the airline and the day, so:

  1. Bathe and change into Ihram at home, at the airport, or on a connecting stop.
  2. Board in Ihram, or carry it in your hand luggage and change on board before the Miqat (aircraft toilets are small, so changing earlier is easier).
  3. Listen for the crew's Miqat announcement, which Saudia and many other airlines make. If yours doesn't, make the intention about an hour before landing to be safe.
  4. Make the intention and begin the Talbiyah.
Connecting via Riyadh, Dubai or Doha?

Your route into Jeddah changes. You will usually cross Qarn al-Manazil or Yalamlam from the east. The rule is the same: be in Ihram before you come level with it.

Flying from Pakistan

Flights from Islamabad, Lahore, Karachi or Peshawar to Jeddah cross the Arabian Peninsula from the east and pass a Miqat (commonly Qarn al-Manazil or Yalamlam, depending on route) shortly before landing. Most pilgrims from Pakistan put on Ihram at home or at the airport before departure, and make the intention on the plane when announced.

Going to Madinah first

If your journey starts in Madinah, for example one of our Madinah-first Hajj 2027 journeys, you do not enter Ihram on the flight. Madinah is outside the Miqat.

When you leave Madinah for Makkah, you pass Dhul Hulayfah. Many groups stop at Masjid Dhul Hulayfah (Abyar Ali) on the way. If you are taking the Haramain high-speed train, the line passes the Miqat soon after leaving, so bathe, change into Ihram at your hotel, and make the intention before or as the train departs.

If you pass the Miqat without Ihram

If you realise you have crossed it, the safest option is to go back to a Miqat and enter Ihram there. If you enter Ihram after passing it, most scholars say a sacrifice (dam) is due. Ask your group scholar as soon as you land.

Questions

Can I put on Ihram in Jeddah?

Not if you are flying in from abroad for Umrah or Hajj. Jeddah lies inside the Miqat boundary, so you must enter Ihram before landing. Residents of Jeddah enter Ihram from home.

Can I wear Ihram for the whole flight?

Yes. You can wear the clothing from departure and only make the intention near the Miqat. The rules of Ihram begin with the intention.

Where is the Miqat on the Haramain train from Madinah?

The train passes Dhul Hulayfah shortly after leaving Madinah, so be in Ihram before you board and make the intention as it departs.
